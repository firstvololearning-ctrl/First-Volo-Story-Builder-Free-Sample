📖 About First Volo Story Builder

Free Sample

This free sample includes the same Story Builder tools and supports as the full version, using a smaller selection of story cards. The full version expands the content library with additional characters, settings, problems, feelings, plans, and items.

First Volo Story Builder is an interactive storytelling tool designed to make narrative development, language organization, sentence formulation, and creative writing more visible, structured, and engaging.

Welcome Story Supports Challenge Planner Save Print Tips

👋 Welcome

Story Builder helps students generate ideas, organize their thinking, develop narrative language, and write complete stories using engaging visual supports.

Students can generate story elements, organize ideas, receive different levels of language support, write their stories, and save or print their work.

🎲 Build a Story

Roll all story elements at once or roll individual cards to change only one part of the story.

  • Character
  • Setting
  • Problem
  • Feeling
  • Plan
  • Item

The free sample includes a smaller selection of cards in each category. The full version includes an expanded library for many more possible story combinations.

⚙️ Customize Story Elements

Educators can show or hide individual story categories to match student needs. A Resolution card can also be included for complete story planning.

💭 Language Supports

🏷 Show Image Labels

Displays the name of each selected image to support vocabulary, spelling, and word retrieval.

📚 Story Vocabulary

Automatically creates a vocabulary list using the selected story elements.

✏ Sentence Support

Choose the amount of support students need:

  • Off
  • Open Prompts
  • Sentence Starters
  • Generated Starters

Generated sentence starters automatically use the selected story elements.

💭 Thinking Prompts

Thinking Prompts encourage students to think more deeply about character motivation, emotions, problem solving, cause and effect, and story organization.

⭐ Story Challenge

Add an optional language challenge to a story.

  • Off
  • Educator Challenge Pool
  • Educator Choice

Challenges can target vocabulary, complex sentences, social thinking, and narrative language.

Students reveal the challenge by flipping a mystery card.

🗂 Interactive Story Planner

The Story Planner automatically copies selected story elements into a graphic organizer where students can organize their ideas before writing.

  • Character
  • Setting
  • Problem
  • Feeling
  • Plan
  • Item
  • Resolution

Planner prompts automatically adjust to match the selected sentence support level.

💾 Save & Open Stories

Story Builder automatically saves student work in the browser while students write.

Stories can also be saved as First Volo Story Builder files that preserve:

  • Story title
  • Writing
  • Planner notes
  • Story selections
  • Supports
  • Story Challenge settings

🖨 Print or Export as PDF

Create a printable Story Planner that can include:

  • Story images
  • Image labels
  • Vocabulary
  • Sentence supports
  • Graphic organizer pages
  • Writing paper
  • The student’s typed draft

💡 Tips for Educators

Beginning Writers

  • Use fewer story elements.
  • Turn on labels and vocabulary.
  • Use Open Prompts.

More Advanced Writers

  • Use all story elements.
  • Add Story Challenges.
  • Turn off supports for independent writing.
